The **Zinc Sulphate Market** is experiencing significant expansion as agriculture, pharmaceuticals, animal nutrition, and industrial sectors increasingly adopt zinc sulphate for diverse applications. In agriculture, zinc sulphate is widely used to correct zinc-deficient soils, supporting crop yield, quality, and disease resistance. Its applications across soil fertilizers, foliar sprays, and seed coatings are gaining importance as farmers focus on sustainable agriculture, soil health, and higher productivity. The pharmaceutical and healthcare sectors are also driving demand, as zinc sulphate is used in dietary supplements and therapeutic formulations to address zinc deficiencies and support immune function and overall health.

Growing demand from the animal feed industry, expanding industrial applications, and increasing use in water treatment, electroplating, dyeing, pigments, and chemical manufacturing are creating substantial opportunities for market growth. Zinc sulphate supports livestock growth, reproduction, and immunity, aligning with rising demand for meat, dairy, and poultry products. Meanwhile, industrial modernization is increasing demand for high-purity zinc sulphate across manufacturing and environmental applications. However, high production costs, including expenses associated with zinc extraction, processing, purification, energy, and advanced equipment, remain a key challenge, particularly in price-sensitive markets. Nevertheless, expanding applications across agriculture, healthcare, animal nutrition, and industrial sectors are expected to sustain market expansion throughout the forecast period.
